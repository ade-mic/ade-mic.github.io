Research

PhD, Agricultural Process Engineering

Universiti Putra Malaysia · Oct 2025 to present

Non-destructive detection of internal disorders in tropical fruit using hyperspectral imaging, thermal imaging and machine learning. Work includes Python and R pipelines for spectral preprocessing, feature selection, classification and model validation.

MSc, Agricultural Process Engineering

Universiti Putra Malaysia · 2021 to 2024

Finite element modelling of the mechanical behaviour of papaya fruit, with geometry from 3D laser scanning, to reduce mechanical damage during postharvest handling.
